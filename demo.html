The test screen

This is the villa as your co-producing team sees it. It's live, not a menu: the cast idles, the fire flickers, and every move happens by tapping the scene.

  1. Explore the villa. Tap a place in the scene (the pool, the fire pit, a window) or a chip in the bar under it to go inside. The map button shows labels if you want them. Tap the time button to move the day along: islanders move around, and some actions only work at certain times. Inside each place you'll overhear live chatter and can make things happen. The clipboard button logs every moment filmed today.
  2. Open a file. Tap any contestant. Their folder slides up with what you know and what you don't.
  3. Producer's Eye. Tap the eye. Pink lines are attraction, gold dotted lines are friendship, red dashed lines are rivalry. A flickering line means trust is low. Look at Kai.
  4. Producer chat. Pull someone to the confessional and ask a question. One of them reveals a hidden trait.
  5. Set up a date. In a file, choose "Pick for a date", tap a second contestant, then pick a postcard. It lands on the clipboard.
  6. Villa phone. Send a text and watch every phone in the villa buzz.
  7. Visit the other places. The bar under the villa switches between the Villa, the Production Trailer (pitch, react, vote, chat with your co-producer, flip your secret agenda), the Casting Van (pick a region and a route, meet three candidates per stop, observe, interview, test chemistry with an islander, pitch with perks, ask for referrals), the Props Department (buy moves; premium ones are gold), and the Edit Bay (reorder clips, pick takes and music, give hero or villain edits, watch the audience react).
  8. Watch tonight's episode. A 30-second sample episode built from your date, with a confessional, a bombshell, and a freeze-frame.

Everything here is drawn in code as a placeholder. In the real game the same scene is painted, stylized 3D, and the sample episode becomes a full 2-to-3-minute show.

Dialogue library

Every line is written for a personality, so a schemer and a sweetheart never sound alike. The test screen, the confessional, and the sample episode all pull from this library, so each tap and each viewing plays differently. Draft 1, for your review.

Style notes

Golden hour is the signature: peach skies, white stucco, turquoise water, lanterns at night. The cast is fashionable, upright, and expressive, in the spirit of Zootopia, with three shared body types so any two can be a believable couple.
