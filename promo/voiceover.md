## Two voice directions

1. **Movie trailer.** Deep, slow, big pauses. Lets the music do the comedy.
2. **Reality-TV narrator.** Dry, quick, a little sarcastic, British or Scottish accent. Funnier, closer to the show it parodies. Use an original voice, not an imitation of a real narrator.

The second probably fits the audience better. Try both.

## ElevenLabs settings to start from

- Stability around 40%, similarity around 75%, style exaggeration around 30%.
- Generate 3 takes per line and pick the best.
- Export MP3 or WAV. The music will be ducked under the voice when they're mixed.
